    Voters who skipped the 2026 primaries, tagged by the most recent 2020, 2022 or 2024 primary they voted in early
    Read with care: this is less reliable than the 2026-only view above. Older primary choices are weaker signals: people switch parties, cross over to vote in the other party's primary, and move. The 2020–2024 lists also cover early primary voters only; the state didn't publish election-day primary voter lists for those years. It still shows nothing about how anyone is voting for Senate.
